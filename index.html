Research: Physics-Inspired AI Systems

My research explores physics-inspired representations for AI systems, including Hamiltonian-based formulations, embedding geometry, semantic similarity, and hybrid classical-quantum methods. I study how mathematical structures from physics can help analyze language embeddings, improve representation quality, and support more robust reasoning in AI systems. This direction connects my work in NLP, retrieval, and quantum-aware machine learning without claiming immediate quantum advantage.
