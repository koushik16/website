Research

My research explores how quantum computing methods can be used to represent and analyze language and machine learning embeddings. I work on hybrid classical-quantum approaches where classical embeddings are encoded into quantum states, compared using quantum similarity measures, and studied through Hamiltonian-based representations. The goal is to understand whether quantum formalisms can reveal deeper structure in semantic representations and support future quantum-aware AI systems.
